The D12 SRD is the core rules for the D12 System. It is used to craft characters and adventures for groups of four to ten. Its goal is to be a resolution mechanic and a customizable system to promote strategic and cooperative roleplay.

# Player Facing

All dice rolls that directly effect the characters or that are initiated by a character's action is rolled by the players.

# Cooperative

The first goal of the system is to encourage cooperative play between players. Keeping players engaged and invested in other player actions is the desired outcome.

# Strategic

The system is designed to use a Battlemap, Chasemap, or Hexmap to provide players clear understanding of conflicts and make meaningful choices to overcome challenges.

# Roleplay

The system supports levels of roleplay depending on the Player's comfort with it. Either by being rewarded for embodying a character or having the option to rely on the character's capabilities as fits the comfort level of the Player. Characters have resources that can be used inside and outside of combat.
