As the game progresses the [[Game Master]] should award [[Milestone]]s. But they are not the only way the [[Character]]s advance.

# Training

With a combination of [[Coin]], [[Downtime]], and [[Research]] the [[Player]] should have plenty of opportunities to add new [[Feature]]s and [[Enigma]]s to their [[Character]]. If they are not, the [[Game Master]] should supplement them with [[Empowered Item]]s or more [[Coin]], [[Downtime]], and access to [[Primer]]s.

# Increasing Tier

As the [[Character]]s earn [[Milestone]]s they rank up their [[Tier]].  Every time they increase Tier they can increase 1 [[Stat]] by +1, the [[Proficient]] [[Skill]]s bonuses becomes the new [[Tier]], and increase their [[Maximum HP]] by their [[Archetype]]'s [[Base HP]] + their [[Fortitude]]. With the increased Tier they should receive a free [[Archetype]] [[Feature]] of the new [[Tier]]. With the increase their challenges and rewards should increase.
