# Spotlight

The Character that is currently acting has the Spotlight. If one Player is dominating the Spotlight the [[Game Master]] should make an active effort to encourage those who have not acted to take the Spotlight.

This can be as strict as an Initiative system where a Player cannot act until all other Players have acted. Or it can be informally tracking who has acted and interjecting with prompts to other Players asking if they have an idea how they want to contribute. 

# [[Prolonged Act]]s

There are acts that take longer than a round of Conflict will allow. Generally taking one or more minutes. If a [[Feature]] uses a Prolonged Act, such as Ritual Casting, it will indicate how long the move will take.

That is not to say that Prolonged Acts cannot be done during a Conflict. Every Turn an Action is required to maintain the Prolonged Act, and taking damage requires a [[Concentration Roll]] to maintain focus. 

# [[Breather]]

A [[Character]] can take a Breather by spending 10 minutes recovering from combat. If the character makes a [[Defense]] or a [[Strength]], [[Dexterity]], or Fortitude Roll they do not get the benefits of the Breather.

> ###### Combat Medicine
> 
> During a Breather a character can use a charge of a Healers [[Kit]] to make an [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] [[Proactive Roll]] against a creature's current HP. As a [[Success]] all [[Bleeding]] conditions end at the beginning of the Breather and the [[Creature]] heals 1d6 [[HP]] at the end of the Breather. As a [[Perk]] it heals 6 HP instead. As a [[Complication]] the creature is [[Blighted]]. And as a [[Failure]] they do not heal at all and the Medical Kit [[Charge]] is wasted.

# [[Day's Rest]]

Day's Rests are at least 8 hours without [[Conflict]] in which the [[Character]]s rest and do light activity like setting up camp, cooking, sleeping, light exercise, and reading. At least 6 of those hours need to be a [[Comfortable Rest]]. The rest can be taken up by watches, eating, preparing camp, crafting, or researching.

## [[Comfortable Rest]]

A Comfortable Rest requires protection from the weather, not wearing armor in an warm place, and a [[Day's Ration]] worth of food and water. To negate cold weather they will need a Bedroll. To negate rain or snow when they don’t have shelter they will need a Tent or to construct a shelter. To cook a Day's Ration requires a fire (Day's Rations that don't require a fire cost 3 times the Coin). If all three can't be met, the [[Character]] is [[Blighted]]. If the character does not expend a [[Day's Ration]], they are [[Sickened]] the next day.

> ###### Weather
> 
> If the weather is clear and warm the characters don't need a bedroll or shelter. If it is raining they need just shelter. If it is windy they just need a bedroll. If it is snowing they need both a bedroll and shelter.

### Constructing Shelter

To construct shelter characters make a[[ Complex Roll]] against a Target Number based on the availability of resources taking 3 hours. Each [[Character]] that contributes reduces the time by 1 hour. With [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] to know where to find resources in the environment, [[Strength]] + [[Craft]] to gather the materials (a Woodcrafting [[Kit]] gives an [[Edge]]), and [[Aptitude]] + [[Craft]] to build the shelter. On a [[Success]] it resists weather to keep the [[Character]]'s dry. As a [[Perk]] it is warm enough to negate the cold. As a [[Complication]] the [[Character]]s trigger a [[Conflict]]. On a [[Failure]] the [[Character]]s don't find enough resources for shelter.

### Natural Healing

Taking a [[Comfortable Rest]] heals the [[Character]]s' 1D6 + [[Body]] per [[Tier]] worth of [[HP]], always healing 1 HP. With a [[Charge]] from a Healers [[Kit]] it removes 1 level of [[Blighted|Blight]] of the [[Player]]'s choice and heals 6 [[HP]] per [[Character]] [[Tier]] instead.

If the character can only meet 2 of the requirements for a [[Comfortable Rest]] they can still recover [[HP]], but cannot remove a [[Blighted|Blight]]. If they can only meet 1 they cannot heal [[HP]] or remove a [[Blighted|Blight]].

## Crafting

During a [[Day's Rest]] a character can spend an hour while not sleeping to craft [[Charge]]s of a crafting [[Feature]] that become inert at the beginning of the next [[Day's Rest]].


## [[Primer]] Research

A character can use an hour of their Day's Rest while not sleeping to research with a [[Primer]] by expending 1 [[Charge]] and making an [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] [[Proactive Roll]] against the [[Tier]] of the [[Primer]]'s [[Target Number]]. On a [[Success]] the researcher gains the [[Research Die]] worth of [[Research Point]]. As a [[Perk]] they gain the maximum [[Research Point]] the [[Research Die]] allows. As a [[Complication]] you have a [[Hindrance]] on rolls when using that [[Primer]] until you [[Success|Succeed]] on a subsequent Primer Research Roll. On a failure the [[Primer]] doesn't have the any further knowledge you need and is useless for your current study, but can be used again once you spend your current [[Research Point]] and choose a new topic. If they attempt a second roll during the same Day’s Rest they have a Hindrance on their Roll.

## Reset Features

Many [[Feature]]s and [[Empowered Item]]s reset their [[Charge]]s on a Day's Rest.

# [[Downtime]] Activities

When [[Character]]s are not adventuring, out of [[Conflict]], and living their lives they can take Downtime Activities. Downtime Activities take a week and have a cost associated with the [[Character]]'s [[Lifestyle]]. Typically Downtime Activities involve a [[Complex Roll]] with its Target Number determined by the Characters' [[Lifestyle]], the [[Tier]] of the Challenge, or the Challenge Rating of a target [[Creature]]. Multiple Characters can spend their [[Downtime]] to participate together in the same [[Downtime Activity]] unless it states otherwise, either to make a Roll or assist in a Roll. Some Activities may have consequences, those consequences that lead to [[Conflict]]s do not interrupt the [[Downtime]], but can occur at the end of that Downtime. Some tasks may take multiple [[Downtime Activities]] to complete.

Downtime has to take place in a Settlement that can support the chosen [[Lifestyle]]. Downtimes that interact with the [[Character]]'s [[Lifestyle]] are limited to the Settlement level. Characters can maintain a [[Lifestyle]] one level above the max [[Lifestyle]] by paying twice the desired [[Lifestyle]]'s cost.

## [[Lifestyle]] [[Downtime Expense]]s

Characters are accustomed to a certain lifestyle's comforts and standing in society that has an associated Coin cost and grants Modifiers to the [[Character]] to the [[Character]] for the [[Downtime]] or next [[Adventure]].

|      [[Lifestyle]]      | Coin   |
| :-----------------: | ------ |
|    **[[Poor]]**     | 10     |
| **[[Comfortable]]** | 100    |
|   **[[Wealthy]]**   | 1,000  |
|    **[[Noble]]**    | 10,000 |

- **[[Poor]]** The minimum amount a person needs to afford a cot, a roof, and food for a week. [[Social]] [[Roll]]s with Wealthy and Noble individuals have a [[Hindrance]] during the next [[Downtime]] or [[Adventure]].
- **[[Comfortable]]** Provides steady housing, easy access to food, and it regularly able to bathe for a week. [[Social]] [[Roll]]s with Noble individuals have a [[Hindrance]] during the next [[Downtime]] or [[Adventure]].
- **[[Wealthy]]** Requires the employment of others to maintain a high quality of food, clothing, and housing that can impress guests. [[Social]] and [[Stealth]] [[Roll]]s to pass among Poor individuals have a [[Hindrance]] during the next [[Downtime]] or [[Adventure]]. [[Social]] [[Roll]]s against a Poor individual has an [[Edge]].
- **[[Noble]]** Noble requires the employment of others and maintenance of a territory with regular expenditures on food, clothing, and accessories that impress the local community. [[Social]] and [[Stealth]] [[Roll]]s to pass as Poor or Comfortable individuals have a [[Hindrance]] during the next [[Downtime]] or [[Adventure]]. [[Social]] [[Roll]]s against a Poor or Comfortable individual has an [[Edge]].

## Designing [[Downtime Activity|Downtime Activities]]

While there are a wide range of [[Downtime Activity|Downtime Activities]] available, not all of them will fit what the [[Player]]s want their [[Character]]s to spend their time on. In this case it is on the [[Game Master]] to design the Downtime around what the Player wants to achieve.

### Time

If the task can be completed in a week then it is best to use a [[Complex Roll]] to determine the outcome. If the goal will take more than a week to achieve, treat the task as [[Research]] and for each Downtime designate a [[Roll]] that adds points to that [[Research]].

### Participants

Some [[Downtime Activity|Downtime Activities]] will support multiple characters working on the same Downtime. In this case choose a Lead for the Downtime who Rolls and allow the other participants to Assist in one Roll. Depending on the Complex Roll, different players can take on each Rolls, in that case they can either Roll or Assist.

### Difficulty

The [[Target Number]] for a Downtime is determined either by the [[Lifestyle]] of the Downtime, the [[Tier]] of the target, or the [[Challenge Rating]] of the target.

### Rewards

If the [[Downtime Activity]] has a monetary reward, the reward should generally correspond to the [[Target Number ]]of the task or the targeted [[Lifestyle]].

## Sample [[Downtime Activity|Downtime Activities]]

### Business

[[Player]]s may want to run businesses for may reasons:

- A place to better sell their treasure from their Adventures.
- To ensure they have a facility for crafting during [[Downtime Activity]]s.
- A way to integrate them into their clientele's good favor.
- An additional source of income while they are adventuring.
- A way to gain an Edge when [[Gather Information|Gathering Information]].
- Provide a legitimate front for illicit activities.

Businesses have an owner, target clientele [[Lifestyle]], and Employees. The business is generally self sustaining, providing the owner a profit based on the Downtime's [[Roll]]. Other [[Character]]s can provide an [[Edge]] on the Running a Business's [[Roll]] if they have the same [[Lifestyle]] as the clientele and spend their [[Downtime]] assisting.

#### Creating a Business

Creating a business requires an investment of 10 times the target clientele's [[Lifestyle]] [[Downtime Expense]]. The clientele's [[Lifestyle]] cannot exceed the level that a [[Settlement]] will support.

#### Run a Business

Running a business will provide a level of reward equal to the target [[Lifestyle]] of the clientele with three [[Aptitude]] + [[Social]] [[Complex Roll]] against the [[Target Number]] of the [[Lifestyle]]. On a [[Success]] it provides the owner twice the clientele's [[Downtime Expense]] and supports a set amount of employees. As a [[Perk]] it provides the owner three times the target clientele's [[Downtime Expense]] instead. As a [[Complication]] the business provides the owner only half of the target clientele's [[Downtime Expense]]. On a [[Failure]] the business requires half the target clientele's [[Downtime Expense]] to remain open and pay its employees.

> ###### Employees
> For each tier of target clientele the business needs employees. Each level of [[Lifestyle]] the clientele targets supports the wages of an amount of employees at each lifestyle.

> |          **[[Poor]]**          |         1         |                          |                      |

#### Expand a Business

To increase the target [[Lifestyle]] of clientele the business caters to requires the character to spend five times the expense of the target [[Lifestyle]] and make a [[Complex Roll]] of [[Will]] + [[Social]] to gather the new clientele (if you have a Ally of the target lifestyle you gain an [[Edge]] on this roll),  [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] to negotiate with higher class suppliers for goods and attract new employees, and [[Aptitude]] + [[Craft]] to find a better location or improve the existing one with. As a [[Perk]] it only costs half as much to expand. As a [[Complication]] it doesn’t produce funds during the next [[Downtime]]. As a [[Failure]] the expansion fails but still costs the same.

### Carousing

To carouse a [[Character]] spends additional [[Coin]] based on the [[Lifestyle]] of who they are carousing among then makes a [[Complex Roll ]]of [[Aptitude]] + [[Social]] to find the best parties, [[Will]] + [[Social]] make friends, and a [[Wits]] + [[Social]] to be entertaining. On a [[Success]] you gain a contact of that social class that can be called upon in the future (Or they may call upon the [[Character]]). As a [[Perk]] the characters gain the "Popular" Title that grants an [[Edge]] [[Social]] [[Roll]]s with that social class and the local area until the end of their next [[Downtime]]. As a [[Complication]] gain the "Boorish" Title that grants a [[Hindrance]] when dealing with that social class in the local area until their next [[Success|Successful]] Carousing. On a [[Failure]] something goes wrong, they are arrested for a minor offense requiring spending their [[Lifestyle]] [[Downtime Expense]] a second time and they gain a [[Hindrance]] on their next Carousing attempt.

### Case a Target

The [[Character]]s can attempt to prepare to infiltrate a location by observing it from the outside or cultivating contacts within it with a [[Complex Roll]] against a Target Number based on the defenses of the location with a [[Aptitude]] + [[Senses]] roll to scout the location, a [[Wits]] + [[Senses]] roll to determine guard patterns or a [[Will]] + [[Social]] to get that information out of someone familiar with the location, and a [[Wits]] + [[Stealth]] to probe the defenses to validate the information gathered. On a [[Success]] you get a rough map of the target location and information on the habits of its inhabitants. As a [[Perk]] the party will not need to make [[Roll]]s to enter the target location. As a [[Complication]] the location will be on high alert aware someone is attempting to infiltrate the location when the party attempts to enter it. On a [[Failure]] the characters are not able to gain any information.

### Crafting Alchemical Consumables

To craft Alchemical [[Consumable]]s requires the Formula to create it, an Alchemical [[Facility]], and make a [[Complex Roll]] of [[Aptitude]] + [[Craft]] three times spending [[Enhanced Material]]s whose cost is based on the [[Tier]] of the item, their [[Potency]] being set by the highest [[Roll]]. On a [[Success]] the [[Character]] crafts 6 items. As a [[Perk]] they craft 9 instead. On a [[Failure]] they produce nothing.

| :--: | :-----------: | :-----------: |
|  1   |       6       |      100      |
|  2   |      10       |     1,000     |
|  3   |      14       |    10,000     |
|  4   |      18       |    100,000    |

> ###### Alchemical Complications
> 
> Depending on the type of consumable crafting them may cause one of the following complications.
>
> - **Blinding** Add [[Blinded]] [[Condition]] to the consumable target.
> - **Diluted** Reduce the [[Diminishing TN]] by 2 every round instead of 1.
> - **Dwindling** Becomes inert after next [[Downtime]] or [[Adventure]].
> - **Explosion** Cause an explosion dealing 1d6 damage per [[Tier]] of [[Consumable]], damaging the [[Facility]] being used to create it, requiring 25% of the construction cost of the [[Facility]] per [[Tier]] of the [[Consumable]] to repair it.
> - **Toxic** Add [[Sickened]] [[Condition]] to the consumable target.
> - **Wasteful** Expend twice the Material Cost.

### Crafting Useful Items

To craft a [[Useful Item]], [[Weapon]], or [[Armor]] requires a successful a [[Moderate]] [[Aptitude]] + [[Craft]] [[Roll]] for every 100 Coin the item costs rolled once a [[Downtime]]. It requires an appropriately furnished workshop to work in. As a [[Perk]] the attempt counts as two [[Success|Successes]]. As a [[Complication]] the attempt damages the item and reduces the final sell price by 25 [[Coin]] each time. On a [[Failure]] the project doesn’t progress.

### Crafting Empowered Items

To craft an [[Empowered Item]] requires the [[Formula]] to create it, an appropriate [[Facility]], and [[Enhanced Material]]s whose cost is based on the [[Tier]]. One of those enhanced materials must be harvested from a non-beast creature thematically linked to the item of the same tier as the item. That material can be purchased with half of the Material Cost of the item.

Every [[Downtime]] the character makes a [[Complex Roll]] of [[Aptitude]] + [[Enigmas]], to channel the primal energies, [[Aptitude]] + [[Craft]] to follow the steps to craft the item, and [[Dexterity]] + [[Craft]] to perform the fine actions necessary for precise work. Other [[Character]]s cannot use their [[Downtime]] to make a [[Roll]], but they can assist on one [[Roll]]. On a [[Success]], the character earns 1 [[Success]]. As a Perk they earn 2 [[Success|Successes]]. As a [[Complication]] they earn no successes.

### Experimentation

As a [[Downtime]] action the researcher can earn [[Research Point]] through Experimentation with a [[Complex Roll]] against a [[Target Number]] based on the [[Tier]] of the subject [[Feature]] of [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] to plan the attempt, the [[Roll]] for the [[Feature]] to attempt it, and [[Aptitude]] + the [[Feature]]'s [[Skill]] to interpret the experiment. On a [[Success]] they earn the [[Research Point]]s based on the [[Feature]]'s [[Tier]]. As a [[Perk]] double the earned [[Research Point]]s. As a [[Complication]] earn half the [[Research Point]]s. On a [[Failure]] learn nothing.

### Finding an [[Empowered Item]] to Purchase

To track down the current owner of a specific [[Empowered Item]] who could be convinced to sell the party an item the characters need to make a [[Complex Roll ]]against a [[Difficulty]] based on the Tier of the item. The size of the [[Settlement]] you are searching determines the max [[Tier]] an item can be found in. They make an [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] to research who might have the item, [[Aptitude]] + [[Social]] to determine what the owner may want in exchange for the item, and [[Will]] + [[Social]] roll to make the owner willing to sell the item. As a [[Perk]] the owner is willing to sell it for coin alone. On a [[Success]] the owner wants something in addition to [[Coin]], a favor, or a lesser [[Tier]] item. As a [[Complication]] the owner will only sell in exchange for a similarly [[Tier]] item or task. On a [[Failure]] they refuse to sell.

### Gambling

To spend a [[Downtime]] gambling the [[Character]] must make a [[Complex Roll]] with a [[Target Number]] based on the [[Lifestyle]] of their opponents. Gambling requires risking an additional week's [[Downtime Expense]]s of the [[Lifestyle]] that you are Gambling among. To find a profitable game requires a [[Aptitude]] + [[Social]], to strategize the game takes a [[Wits]] + [[Knowledge]], and to influence the game through cheating requires a [[Dexterity]] + [[Stealth]] or through bluffing requires a [[Will]] + [[Social]]. On a [[Success]] the character keeps their risked coin as well as winning half of what they risked. As a [[Perk]] instead they earn twice of what they risked. As a [[Complication]] they lose half their risked coin. And if they [[Failure|Fail]] they lose all they risked.

### [[Gather Information]]

To find information on a person, place, or thing the characters need to make a [[Complex Roll]] against a [[Target Number]] based on how secret or obscure the information is with an [[Aptitude]] + [[Social]] to find who or where the information might be, a [[Dexterity]] + [[Stealth]] to sneak in or a [[Will]] + [[Stealth]] to fake their way to get the knowledge, and a [[Will]] + [[Social]] to convince someone to give the knowledge or a [[Aptitude]] + [[Senses]] to extract the knowledge from documents. On a [[Success]] the [[Character]]s learn three facts about the target. As a [[Perk]] the characters gain two more facts. As a [[Complication]] they get caught if it is secured knowledge or learn an additional falsehood if it is not. On a [[Failure]] they find nothing.

> ###### Bribery
> 
> Bribery can be used to gain an [[Edge]] on the [[Social]] [[Roll]]s depending on the [[Lifestyle]] of the target holding the information. Paying a [[Downtime Expense]] provides an [[Edge]] on one [[Roll]] of the [[Complex Roll]].

### Investigate Lore

To find the [[Feature]]s of a [[Creature]] or [[Empowered Item]] they make a [[Complex Roll]] against a [[Target Number]] based on the [[Tier]] of the target with [[Aptitude]] + [[Social]] to find the source of the information, [[Will]] + [[Social]] to convince the holder to to give the Lore or a [[Wits]] + [[Stealth]] to extract the Lore, and [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] roll to interpret the Lore or a [[Will]] + [[Social]] to convince someone else to interpret it for you. On a [[Success]] you learn a [[Feature]] of an [[Empowered Item]] or 1d4 [[Feature]]s of the [[Creature]] (Increase the [[Die Type]] for every [[Tier]] of [[Creature]]). As a [[Perk]] you gain additional information that will give you an [[Edge]] in finding or interacting with the focus of your investigation or all the [[Empowered Item]]'s or [[Creature]]'s [[Feature]]s. As a [[Complication]] someone else gets the information before you, you get additional wrong information, or you fail to gain the information but learn where the information may be. On a Failure you get no information.

### Pick Pockets

To take coin or treasures of associated value from targets out and about in the world choose the [[Lifestyle]] level of your targets and make a [[Complex Roll]] against the target's [[React]] [[Defense]] with [[Wits]] + [[Social]] to pick out the target, a [[Dexterity]] + [[Stealth]] to sneak the items off of the target, and [[Dexterity]] + [[Stealth]] to escape undetected or or [[Dexterity]] + [[Social]] to distract the target so they don't notice the character. On a [[Success]] the character gains half the [[Downtime Expense]] of the target. As a [[Perk]] you gain an item that leads to a new adventure or a treasure worth half of the take. As a [[Complication]] you get detected but escape and the target is aware of you. If you fail you are caught and arrested by guards or attacked by your target.

### Pit Fighting

To compete in Pit Fighting for a reward, the [[Character]] makes a [[Complex Roll]] against the [[Lifestyle]] [[Target Number ]]of the audience. Every time they [[Roll]], the [[Stat]] in that roll is [[Blighted]]. On each roll as a [[Perk]] they gain [[Edge]] on the next roll and are not [[Blighted]], and as a [[Complication]] they have a [[Hindrance]] on their next [[Roll]]. On a [[Failure]], a second [[Stat]] is [[Blighted]]. They fight with a [[Strength]] + Weapons roll or Strength + Athletics if it is unarmed fighting, then a Fortitude + Athletics to endure damage or Wits + Athletics to avoid it, and Strength + Social or Dexterity + Social to work the crowd. On a [[Success]] they earn twice a week's [[Downtime Expense]] of the chosen audience. As a [[Perk]] the [[Character]] gains an additional half of the reward or gains the "Champion Fighter" [[Title]] until the end of the next [[Downtime]] or [[Adventure]]. As a [[Complication]] the character gains a rival. On a [[Failure]] they gain the “Lost Fighter” [[Title]] until they win a Pit Fight or after 3 [[Downtime]]s or [[Adventure]]s.

> ###### Champion Fighter
> 
> Champion Fighter can give an [[Edge]] on [[Roll]]s to intimidate or frighten foes during then next [[Downtime]] or [[Adventure]]. Each time the [[Character]] earns this title, the [[Title]] lasts an additional total [[Downtime]]. Upon a second award, the effect lasts for 2 [[Downtime]]s or [[Adventure]]s. 

### Research with a [[Primer]]

The researcher expends three charges of the [[Primer]] and make an [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] three times against the [[Tier]] of the [[Primer]]'s [[Target Number]]. On a [[Success]] the researcher gains the  [[Primer]]'s [[Research Die]] roll * 5 worth of [[Research Point]]s. As a [[Perk]] they gain the maximum value of the [[Research Die]] * 5. As a [[Complication]] you gain the [[Primer]]'s [[Research Die]] * 5 worth of [[Research Point]]s but have a [[Hindrance]] on [[Roll]]s using that [[Primer]] until you [[Success|Succeed]] using the [[Primer]]. On a [[Failure]] the [[Primer]] doesn't have the knowledge you need and is useless for further use in your current study.

> ###### [[Primer]]s
> 
> Primers contain information on a group of topics and have a [[Research Die]] ranging from 1D4 at [[Tier]] 1 to 1D10 at [[Tier]] 4 depending on the [[Tier]] of the Primer, and an amount of [[Charge]]s equal to 10 times the [[Tier]]. Once the researcher reaches 100 * the Primer's [[Tier]] worth of [[Research Point]]s, that primer can no longer be used to gain new [[Research Point]]s, until the current research is complete. Every time a Primer is used by a [[Character]], halve the amount of [[Research Point]]s the Primer can provide for their next Research.

> If the [[Character]] can attract a trainer who already knows the [[Feature]] and is an [[Allied|Ally]], they gain an [[Edge]] on their Roll. If the trainer is not an [[Allied|Ally]] they can be commissioned to train the [[Character]] the Instruction Cost in [[Coin]].
> 
> To find a Trainer the [[Character]] uses a [[Downtime]] to make a [[Complex Roll]] against a [[Target Number]] based on the [[Tier]] of the [[Feature]]. The size of a [[Settlement]] determines if a Trainer is available based on the [[Tier]] of the [[Feature]]. To start they need to make an [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] to research who might have the [[Feature]], [[Aptitude]] + [[Social]] to determine what the trainer wants in exchange, and [[Will]] +[[Social]] to make the trainer take the time to train the [[Character]].

### Training a [[Pet]]

Training a captured beast to make them a Pet has a [[Target Number ]]of 6 plus the beast’s [[Challenge Rating]]. First the [[Character]] must change the beast's [[Demeanor]] towards them to [[Allied|Ally]]. They can attempt this once per [[Downtime]] by making a [[Complex Roll]] of three [[Will]]+[[Social]]. On a [[Success]] they increase the [[Demeanor]] by one rank. As a [[Perk]] it increases by two ranks. As a [[Complication]] there is no change. And on a [[Failure]] the [[Demeanor]] reduces one rank.

Once the beast is an [[Allied|Ally]], training them to accept commands takes the beast’s [[Challenge Rating]] worth of [[Downtime]]. At the end of each [[Downtime]] make a [[Proactive Roll]] of [[Will]] + [[Social]] vs 6 plus the beast’s [[Challenge Rating]]. On a [[Success]] gain a training. As a [[Perk]] the [[Downtime]] counts as 2 trainings. As a [[Complication]] the Downtime counts as no trainings. And a [[Failure]] removes an existent training. Between trainings, a beast will follow its master but only [[Hide]] or [[Dodge]] in combat.

> ###### [[Battle Trained|Battle Training]] [[Pet]]s for Combat
> 
> Training for to attack or assist a [[Target]] on command takes twice the beast’s [[Challenge Rating]] worth of [[Downtime]]. Make a [[Complex Roll]] of three [[Will]] + [[Social]] vs 6 plus the beast's [[Challenge Rating]]. As a [[Perk]], the [[Downtime]] counts as two trainings. As a [[Complication]] the [[Downtime]] provides no training. And on a [[Failure|Failed]] roll remove two trainings.

### Transcribing a [[Enigma]] into a Codex

Enigmas exist outside simple understanding and logic. As such they need to be translated by the [[Character]] so they can understand it. Scribing an [[Enigma]] from a Scroll or [[Codex]] into a [[Character]]'s [[Codex]] requires an [[Aptitude]] +[[Enigmas]] roll against the a [[Target Number]] based on the [[Tier]] of the [[Enigma]] (Increase the [[Target Number]] by 2 for an [[Enigma]] that doesn't belong to the [[Character]]'s [[Archetype]]). Scribing requires a successful [[Downtime]] activity per [[Tier]] of the [[Enigmas]] [[Proactive Roll]] against the [[Target Number]] each [[Downtime Activity]]. On a [[Success]] they are able to scribe the [[Enigma]] in their [[Codex]]. As a [[Perk]] it takes up half the pages. As a [[Complication]] it takes up its [[Tier]] worth of additional pages. And on a [[Failure]] the pages are ruined and will need to be transcribed again with an [[Edge]] on the three [[Roll]]s. When copying from a [[Codex]] the character wrote the spell in, they have an [[Edge]] on the three [[Roll]]s to copy it.

> ###### Creating [[Enigma]] from [[Research]]
> 
> Once the [[Character]] has enough [[Research]] for an [[Enigma]] they can Transcribe it to their [[Codex]]. As a [[Complication]] the Character needs to research an additional 1/10 of the cost to be able to transcribe the [[Enigma]]. On a [[Failure]] the [[Character]] needs an additional 1/2 of the [[Research Cost]] to attempt to Transcribe the [[Enigma]] again. Each additional failure reduces the [[Research Cost ]]by 1/2 of the previous attempt.

### Transcribing a [[Formula]]

Formulae are instructions to craft items that have [[Feature]]s. They are also scribed in [[Codex]]es. Transcribing only takes a single [[Downtime]] and [[Complex Roll]] of [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] to interpret the Formula, [[Aptitude]] + [[Craft]] to precisely reproduce the instructions, and [[Dexterity]] + [[Craft]] to create samples components vs a [[Target Number]] based on the Formula's [[Tier]] (increase the [[Target Number]] by 2 for a Formula that doesn't match the [[Character]]'s [[Archetype]]).  [[Formula]]e for [[Consumable]]s require half the [[Research Point]]. 

> ###### Creating Formula from [[Research]]
> 
> Once they complete enough [[Research Point]]s they can transcribe the Formula.
