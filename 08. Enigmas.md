Enigmas are supernatural [[Feature]]s that function beyond the mundane world. They are abstractions of manipulating supernatural, arcane, and divine forces. Spells, miracles, and other manifestations of supernatural power are Enigmas.

# [[Enigma Feature]]s

Enigma Features do not have a single effect, but give a [[Creature]] access to many potential named effects, or Enigmas. These could be spells, miracles, or other supernatural effects. Every Enigma Feature includes a [[Casting Stat]] that is used in their [[Casting Roll]] to manifest the Enigma.

## [[Casting Roll]]

To manifest an Enigma the [[Character]] make a [[Casting Stat]] + [[Enigmas]] [[Roll]]. If the effect negatively impacts the [[Target]] of the Enigma, the [[Roll]] is against the Enigma's listed [[Defense]]. If the [[Enigma Feature]] uses [[Burn]] that same [[Roll]] must also meet or surpass the [[Character]]'s current [[Burn]] to manifest.

-  **12 on Die and over [[Defense]] and [[Burn]]** [[Success|Succeeds]] with a [[Perk]].
- **Over or equal to [[Defense]]** and **[[Burn]]** [[Success|Succeeds]].
- **Under [[Defense]]** [[Failure|Fails]].
- **Under [[Burn]]** [[Failure|Fails]] and does not increase [[Burn]].
- **1 on the Die** [[Failure|Fails]] and does increase [[Burn]].

### [[Casting Bonus]]

The combination of the [[Casting Stat]] + [[Enigmas]] is the Casting Bonus and many and Enigmas that are accessible to multiple [[Archetype]]s will refer to it as such.  

> ###### [[Focus]]
> 
> There are [[Feature]]s that are similar to [[Enigma Feature]]s that grant access to Enigma-like named effects, though they use [[Focus]] instead of [[Enigmas]] in their [[Casting Roll]], if they have one. These typically have lesser effects and tend to only [[Target]] the caster. Otherwise they use the same rules to manifest as Enigma Features.

# Requirements for an Enigma

## Manifesting

Generally, to manifest an [[Enigma]], a [[Creature]] has to speak some sort of focusing words in a clear audible voice and be able to make symbols in the air with a [[Foci]] or free hand that is not [[Restrained]]. Some Enigma [[Feature]]s may provide exceptions to these requirements and they will be noted. If any one of these features are not met the [[Casting Roll]] has a [[Hindrance]]. If two or more are missing the spall fails. 

## Foci

A special item that is used by the caster to manifest Enigmas who's shape is thematically linked to the power source of the [[Archetype]].

## Burn

Burn is the current level of strain that the creature must overcome to cast a spell or activate a [[Feature]]. After a [[Day's Rest]], their Burn is set to a base value set by the casting [[Feature]]. If a spell has an effect on a failed roll, that roll still needs to be equal to or greater than the current [[Burn]] to successfully cast. If the spell successfully casts, add the spell’s tier to the caster's current Burn. Failing to roll over the Burn does not increase the Burn except as a [[Complication]]. Then add the spell's tier to the caster's current Burn. [[Cantrip]]s do not add to the caster's Burn, but the [[Casting Roll]]must be equal to or greater than the current Burn to manifest.

## Charges

Some Enigma Features have a set amount of [[Charge]]s that can be spent to manifest Enigmas. These [[Charge]]s typically reset on a [[Day's Rest]] or on a [[Breather]].

## [[Fervor]]

Some Enigma Features spend Fervor to manifest their [[Enigma]]s. Fervor is accrued in [[Conflict]]s as described by the Enigma Feature. 

# Anatomy of an Enigma

While each Enigma manifests a different source of power, the [[Feature]]s they empower have similar components. Their name, a [[Tier]], [[Archetype]]s that it is thematically linked to, a [[Casting Time]], [[Target]], [[Duration]], a description of their effects, higher Tier effects, an Enigma specific [[Perk]], and any special materials or foci that the Enigma requires.

## Tier

Every Enigma has a Tier that determines not only their availabilty but their cost in [[Burn]], [[Fervor]] or [[Charge]]s.

### [[Cantrip]]

Cantrips are Enigmas that do not have a cost associated with them. Typically they increase in power as the [[Character]] increases in [[Tier]]. They will always have a unique [[Perk]].

## [[Casting Time]]

Each Enigma has a casting time that dictate what type of act is required to use it: [[Action]], [[Maneuver]], [[Interaction]], or a [[Prolonged Act]].

> ###### Ritual
> 
> Enigmas with a Ritual casting time can be cast with a [[Prolonged Act]] over a period of time to prevent having to roll against the Enigma's [[Burn]]. Multiple [[Character]]s can be led in the ritual if they are [[Proficient]] in [[Enigmas]]. However, the [[Character]] leading the Ritual can only perform it if their Burn is less than 12. And if a participant takes damage they must make a [[Concentration Roll]] or the Ritual loses the benefit of their participation. Depending on the [[Tier]] of the Enigma, the amount of participants may grant an [[Edge]] to the lead caster's [[Casting Roll]].

## [[Target]]

An Enigma can [[Target]] a single [[Creature]], or all [[Creature]]s within an area. Listed under the Target is also the [[Defense]] that the [[Casting Roll]] is rolled against. If the Enigma

> ###### [[Line of Effect]]
> 
> For an Enigma Feature to effect a [[Target]], spaces need to be unobstructed emanating from the origin. [[Cover]] obstructs emanations even if they are transparent.


## [[Duration]]

If an Enigma doesn't have a duration it ends immediately after being manifested. Otehrwise the Duration describes how long the Enigma lasts. Enigmas end if the caster begins [[Dying]], regardless of Duration.

### [[Concentration]]

As well as having a time limit, an Enigma may require Concentration. A [[Character]] can only Concentrate on one [[Feature]] at a time. If a Concentrating [[Creature]] takes [[Damage]] they must [[Success|Succeed]] on a [[Concentration Roll]] or the Enigma ends.

### Permanent

Permanent spells don't end until the caster dispels them of dies.

### Ongoing

If the Enigma is Ongoing, the [[Casting Roll]] becomes the [[Target Number]] to end the spell. If the [[Target]] is a [[Character]] they make their [[Defense Roll]] at the beginning of their [[Turn]] an on a [[Success]] the Enigma Ends. Otherwise the [[Target Number]] becomes a [[Decreasing TN]] that is overcome once it drops below the [[Target]]'s [[Defense]].
