The core resolution mechanic is the eponymous D12 plus applicable bonuses against a [[Target Number]] ([[Target Number|TN]]). [[Player|Players]] declare what their [[Character]] is attempting, the [[Game Master]] decides on an appropriate [[Target Number]] for the task and the applicable bonuses from the [[Character]]'s [[Stat]]s, [[Skill]]s, and Expertises. Then the [[Player]] rolls to determine if the [[Character]] [[Success|Succeeds]], gains a [[Perk]] or [[Complication]], or [[Failure|Fails]] entirely.

# Scale of Success / Failure

Beyond just [[Success|Succeeding]] or [[Failure|Failing]] an attempt may have additional events that push the story forward.

## [[Perk]]

When a 12 is rolled on the die and the [[Roll]] is over or equal to the [[Target Number]], the action succeeds with an additional effect. This effect will always benefit the character, even if the benefit doesn't directly relate to the Task. It could be discovering a beneficial item or clue, earning a favor from an [[Antagonist]], or has a special effect. 

## [[Complication]]

Complications are not failures, they are just something new going wrong or they introduce a new challenge the [[Character]]s need to overcome. They should always push the story forward and do not have to be a direct outcome of the task. Unless the [[Roll]] is already a [[Failure]], Complications should not negate or take away the [[Character]]'s [[Success]]. 

# Make a [[Roll]]

There are [[Proactive Roll]]s, [[Conflict Roll]]s, [[Defense Roll]]s, or [[Complex Roll]]s each with their own outcome scale.

> ###### [[Edge]]s and [[Hindrance]]s
> If the [[Character]] has transitory advantages on a task either from their position, their roleplay, or other [[Scene Feature]] they have an [[Edge]] on the Task, rolling the D12 twice and taking the higher result.
>
> If the [[Character]] has some transitory disadvantage on a task, they have a [[Hindrance]] rolling the D12 twice and taking the lower result.
>
> No matter how many Edges or Hinderances a Character has, they only make one addition roll. [[Edge]]s and [[Hindrance]]s cancel each other out until only one or none remains.

## [[Proactive Roll]]s

When the [[Player]] proposes a task they want their [[Character]] to attempt, the [[Game Master]] sets a [[Target Number]] and what [[Stat]] + [[Skill]] + applicable [[Expertise]]. The [[Roll]] determines the outcome.

- **12 on Die and over [[Target Number|TN]]** [[Success]] with a [[Perk]].
- **Over or equal to [[Target Number|TN]]** [[Success]].
- **Under [[Target Number|TN]]** [[Success]] with a [[Complication]].
- **1 on the Die** [[Failure]].

## [[Conflict Roll]]s

Features [[Character]]s use in [[Conflict]]s have a listed [[Skill]] + Stat[[ ]]that are rolled against a [[Target]]'s [[Defense]]. [[Expertise]]s may apply to this [[Roll]].
-  **12 on Die and over [[Defense]]** [[Success|Succeeds]] with a [[Perk]].
- **Over or equal to [[Defense]]** [[Success|Succeeds]].
- **Under [[Defense]]** [[Failure|Fails]].
- **1 on the Die** [[Failure|Fails]] and [[Character]] is [[Exposed]].
### [[Conflict Perk]]

[[Weapon]]s have default [[Perk|Perks]] associated with them the [[Player]] can choose one of. Other [[Feature]]s will either use the [[Weapon]]'s [[Perk]] or have a specified [[Perk]].

### [[Conflict Complication]]

The [[Character]] is [[Exposed]] as a complication, unless the target has a [[Defense Complication]] [[Feature]].

## [[Defense Roll]]s

When a [[Character]] defends against a [[Feature]], they add the applicable [[Defense]] to their [[Roll]].

-  **12 on Die and over [[Defense]]** [[Success]] with a [[Defensive Perk]].
- **Over or equal to [[Defense]]** [[Success]].
- **Under [[Defense]]** Failure.
- **1 on the Die** Failure with a [[Defense Complication]].

### [[Defensive Perk]]

The attacker is [[Exposed]] unless the [[Character]] has a [[Feature]] that gives them a [[Defensive Perk]].

### [[Defense Complication]]

By default the [[Character]] is [[Exposed]] unless the attacking [[Antagonist]]'s [[Feature]] has a [[Defense Complication|Complication]]. 

## [[Complex Roll]]s

There are times when a simple [[Roll]] isn’t enough to cover all the aspects of an action. In those cases the [[Player]]s make [[Complex Roll]]s. These entail three different [[Roll]]s, each to achieve a different aspect of the task. [[Roll]]s either succeed if they meet or beat the Complex Roll’s [[Target Number]], or fail if they are below the [[Target Number]]. As a [[Perk]] the [[Roll]] counts as 2 successes, and [[Complication]]s count as 0 successes. On a [[Failure]] the count reduces by -1 successes. Once all three [[Roll]]s have been made total the successes to determine the outcome. 

- **3+ Successes** the action [[Success|Succeeds]] with a [[Perk]].
- **2 Successes** the action [[Success|Succeeds]].
- **1 Success** the action [[Success|Succeeds]] with a [[Complication]].
- **0 or fewer Successes** action fails.

If the task is not being performed by a single [[Character]], the [[Game Master]] should encourage a different [[Player]] making each Roll by granting a Hindrance on subsequent [[Roll]]s by the same [[Character]].

> ###### Assisting
> If another [[Character]] is proficient in the same [[Skill]] or has an applicable [[Expertise]] they can assist the other [[Character]], granting them an [[Edge]] on their Roll within the Complex Roll.

# Other Dice

If you see the keyword [[Roll]] it always is a D12. If another die is needed the [[Die Type]] will be explicitly described.

# [[Stat]]s

Stats are the basic characteristics of the [[Character]]. Every [[Roll]]'s bonus includes only one of them.

## [[Strength]]

Strength is the measure of brute power that a creature has. It is used for striking, lifting, jumping, and other feats of physical exertion. If a character makes an Unarmed Strike they do damage equal to their Strength bonus dealing a minimum of 0 damage.

- Making most [[Weapon]] attacks.
- Breaking something.
- Lifting heavy loads.

## [[Fortitude]]

Fortitude is how durable a creature is. It is a measure of their health and overall durability.

- Endure strenuous activity.
- Ignore pain.

## [[Aptitude]]

Aptitude is how well a creature learns and retains knowledge for later recall. As well, it measures how well the creature can apply its knowledge in the pursuit of a task.

- Learn new [[Feature]]s.
- Interpret [[Research]].
- Problem solving.

## [[Will]]

Will is a creature's strength of will and how well it can express it and maintain it.

- Negotiate with or convince [[Antagonist]]s.
- Intimidate [[Antagonist]]s.
- Maintain focus when distracted or taking injuries.

## [[Dexterity]]

Dexterity is how physically skilled a creature is. It is a measure of fine control and flexibility.

- Fire a [[Ranged]] [[Weapon]].
- Perform acrobatics.
- Wriggle through tight spaces.
- Slip from grasp.
- Precisely manipulate mechanisms

## [[Wits]]

Wits are how well a creature maintains their focus in a stressful situation and how quickly they can react.

- Notice threats.
- Dodge threats
- React with speed.
- Choosing the right moment to trigger something.

# [[Skill]]s

A [[Character]] is [[Proficient]] in a number of Skills. When making a [[Roll]] [[Character]]s include their [[Tier]] in the bonuses if they are [[Proficient]].

## [[Athletics]]

Athletics is the creature's physical prowess. Their ability to focus their strength, their ability to perform feats of acrobatics, and their ability to endure exertion. There are some specific ways these skills are used.

- **Acrobatics** [[Dexterity|Dexterity]] + [[Athletics]] to tumble, or otherwise move acrobatically.
- **Climbing** [[Strength]] +[[Athletics]] to move half the creature's [[Stride]] up a steeply angled or vertical surface.
- **Jumping** [[Strength]] +[[Athletics]] to make a running leap of against the [[Target Number]] of the distance.
- **Swimming** [[Strength]] +[[Athletics]] to move half the creature's [[Stride]] while swimming against the [[Target Number]] of the current.
- **Throwing** [[Strength]] +[[Athletics]] to throw an object.

## [[Craft]]

Craft is a creature's understanding of how to make things and how to break things.

- **Crafting items** [[Aptitude]] +[[Craft]].
- **Determining the value or provenance of an item** [[Aptitude]] +[[Craft]].
- **Pick a lock** [[Dexterity|Dexterity]] + [[Craft]].
- **Sundering an item** [[Strength]] +[[Craft]].

## [[Enigmas]]

Enigmas are the supernatural forces that shape the world. They violate reason and are beyond logic. Each creature has a unique understanding of the Enigmas. Skill in Enigmas is used in spellcasting or other supernatural [[Features]].

## [[Focus]]

Focus is used to focus on a task, ignoring pain, wounds, or other distractions.

- **Use an [[Action]] to Reroll a failed [[Mind]] defense against an ongoing effect**, [[Will]] + [[Focus]].
- **Concentrating on a Enigma** [[Will]] + [[Focus]] against damage taken before [[Soak]] is applied.
- **Holding your breath** [[Fortitude]] + [[Focus]] against damage taken before [[Soak]] is applied..

## [[Knowledge]] 

Knowledge is used for recalling information a creature might know and represents the training they have received.

- **Interpret information** [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]].
- **Strategize under pressure** [[Wits]] + [[Knowledge]]
- **Research new [[Feature]]s** [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]]

## [[Pilot]]

This is a creature's ability to control a [[Mount]], [[Vehicle]], or other conveyance.

- **Ride a [[Mount]]** [[Will]] + [[Pilot]].
- **Drive a [[Vehicle]]** [[Wits]] + [[Pilot]].
- **Shake a pursuer** [[Wits]] + [[Pilot]].

## [[Senses]]

This is a creature's awareness of the world and their surroundings.

- **Notice a threat** [[Wits]] + [[Senses]].
- **Investigate an area** [[Aptitude]] +[[Senses]].

## [[Social]] 

Social is what creatures use to interact and manipulate other creatures

- **Direct an animal** [[Will]] + [[Social]].
- **Gain insight into a creature's mental state or intentions** [[Wits]] + [[Social]].
- **Persuade someone** [[Will]] + [[Social]].
- **Intimidate someone with a show of power** [[Strength]] +[[Social]].

## [[Stealth]]

This is a creature's skill at hiding and otherwise avoiding being seen.

- **Misdirect a foes attention** [[Wits]] + [[Stealth]].
- **Camouflage a creature or object** [[Aptitude]] +[[Stealth]].
- **Slip into cover** [[Dexterity]] + [[Stealth]].

## [[Weapons]] 

This is a creature's prowess with weapons. If a weapon has multiple [[Tag|Tags]] you can choose which [[Stat]] +[[Skill]] combo to use. When dealing damage with a weapon roll, add the [[Stat]] bonus you used in the roll.

- **Most weapon attacks**  [[Strength]] + [[Weapons]] 
- **Weapons with the [[Thrown]] [[Tag]]** [[Strength]] +[[Weapons]] 
- **Weapons with the [[Fine]] [[Tag]]** [[Dexterity]] + [[Weapons]] 
- **Weapons with the [[Finesse]] [[Tag]]** [[Dexterity]] + [[Weapons]] 
- **Weapons with the [[Mechanical]] [[Tag]]** [[Wits]] + [[Weapons]] 

# [[Expertise]]s

Expertises represent broad training or specialties that are not captured by a single skill. Only one Expertise can be added to a [[Roll]] if it applies to the task, granting a +2 bonus. 

_Examples_:

- **Animal Handling** Controlling and training animals.
- **Arcana** Education in the arcane. Including knowledge of Monstrosities, Elementals, and Outsiders.
- **Diplomacy** You are a master of the fine art of coming to consensus.
- **History** Educated in history.
- **Intimidation** Manipulating and controlling creatures through fear.
- **Medicine** Experience with medicine.
- **Mounted Combat** Mastery of controlling a mount while in combat.
- **Nature** Education in the natural world. Including knowledge of Beasts and Plants.
- **Persuasion** Manipulating creatures through force of personality.
- **Profession** Mastery of a specific occupation.
- **Religion** Education in the beliefs and rituals of various religions. Including knowledge about Undead and Outsiders.
- **Unarmed Combat** Mastery of dealing damage unarmed.
- **Weapon** Mastery of a specific weapon.

## Creating [[Expertise]]s

Characters can develop Expertises during play. Those should not be as broad as those gained from [[Background]]s, [[Culture]]s, and [[Archetype]]s.

# [[Tier]]s of Play

The game supports four Tiers of play. These Tiers determine the power level of the [[Character]]s; what [[Feature]]s they have access to, the bonuses to their [[Roll]]s, their [[HP]], what [[Empowered Item]]s they can utilize and the scale of the challenges they face. Typically the [[Character]]s complete two to four [[Adventure]]s before advancing to the next Tier, as the [[Game Master]] decides.

# Safety

Before the game begins the [[Game Master]] should discuss with the [[Player]]s either together or individually to set expectations for what they can expect from your game, what sensitive topics the game may touch on, determine how comfortable each [[Player]] is with those topics, and how they can request a pause in game or a reduction in description while the game is in progress. While you want to surprise your [[Player]]s with events and ramifications, you don’t want to ambush them with something that may turn them off of the game.

If you plan on running a game that may include sensitive topics there are forms available to poll your [[Player]]s on their comfort level with sensitive topics. 

## Trust

Part of roleplaying is making yourself vulnerable to looking foolish, embarrassing yourself, or reacting badly to the scenarios presented. If a [[Player]] doesn’t trust the rest of the group they won’t take risks in their play.

## "I need a pause."

If a [[Player]] says this the [[Game Master]] should pause the game, giving the [[Player]] a minute or more to center themselves. You may want to take them aside and ask what caused the pause so you can adjust your description or the plot accordingly.

## "Could you back off?"

Sometimes as a [[Game Master]] you get into deep description of what is happening in the game, and though it may not step on a sensitive topic, it may be more detailed then they are comfortable with. In this case, in the moment simplify the descriptions to be vaguer, then when there is a break in the action take the [[Player]] aside and discuss what made them uncomfortable.
