The [[Character]] is the vessel through which the [[Player]]s' experience the game and express their [[Agency]] upon it. They should fulfill a fantasy, theme, or tropes the [[Player]] is interested in. Generally all [[Character]]s start at [[Tier]] 1, though the [[Game Master]] may grant [[07. Advancement|Advancements]] during character creation to start at a higher power level.

# Steps to Create a Character

1. Envision the [[Character]] you want to play and write their [[Personality Trait|Personality Traits]].
2. Choose the [[Character]]'s [[Culture]]. Cultures are also a form of [[Expertise]]. Additionally, cultures provide one [[Feature]].
3. Choose the [[Character]]'s [[Background|Backgrounds]]. Backgrounds are also a form of [[Expertise]]. They provide starting equipment and may also include a [[Feature]].
4. Choose the [[Character]]'s [[Ancestry|Ancestries]]. Ancestries provide two [[Feature|Features]]. [[Character]]s of mixed ancestry take one feature from each of their two dominant ancestries.
5. Choose the [[Character]]'s [[Archetype]]. This informs the fantasy, themes, and tropes the character will embody, shaping the [[Feature]]s that fulfill and reinforce them.
6. The [[Archetype]] gives you more equipment, focuses on two primary [[Stat|Stats]], provides your base [[HP]], grants [[Skill|Skills]], and thematic starting equipment.
7. Assign the array of [[Stat]]s +2, +2, +1, +1, +0, -1 and then increase one stat by +1.
8. Choose the [[Character]]'s [[Skill]]s. At [[Tier]] 1 their bonus is +1.
9. Determine your [[Defense|Defenses]] from your [[Stat|Stats]].
10. Look up armor and weapon details.
11. Derive your [[Defense|Defenses]].
12. Write out your Character's [[Action|Actions]], [[Maneuver|Maneuvers]], [[Response|Responses]], [[Interaction|Interactions]], [[Response|Responses]] and their [[Perk|Perks]] with their bonuses and [[Target]]'s [[Defense]] with their bonuses and target Defense for quick reference.
13. Purchase any additional [[#Equipment]] with the [[Character]]'s remaining [[Coin]].

# [[Personality Trait]]s

The [[Character]]'s Personality Traits inform the [[Player]]'s roleplaying, give the [[Game Master]] tools to challenge the [[Character]]s with, and can lead to additional rewards by embodying the traits or sacrificing to overcome them.

> ###### [[Reroll]]s
> 
> By playing to a [[Character]]'s Personality Traits, the [[Game Master]] rewards [[Reroll]]s that can be spent during that session. When the [[Character]] is embodying the trait, the [[Player]] can make an aside comment indicating that they are playing to the [[Drive]], [[Flaw]], or [[Fear]].

## [[Drive]]

What drives the [[Character]]? Why do they adventure? Why didn't they just take the same job as a parent and stay near their home? They should be active goals that are not permanently achievable but can be acted on in the moment. When a [[Character]] sticks to their Drive when there is a cost associated with it, is worth a [[Reroll]].

- To protect those who cannot protect themselves.
- To break the bonds of tyranny.
- To live free and experience the world.
- To repay a debt I can never repay.
- To seek wealth and the pleasures it brings.

## [[Flaw]]

What undermines the [[Character]]? Is this the [[Character]]'s drive taken to extreme? Is it a weakness that the [[Character]] will revert to when pressed? Acting on the Character's Flaw n a situation where it causes complications is the easiest to gain [[Reroll|Rerolls]]. As well, overcoming the Flaw at great cost is worth gaining [[Reroll|Rerolls]].

- I am a braggart that will bite off more than I can chew.
- I care more about enriching myself than the lives of others.
- In the face of overwhelming odds I will abandon my fellows.
- The pursuit of knowledge blinds me to the plight of others.

## [[Fear]]

What does the [[Character]] fear? What will dissuade them from their goal? What holds them back and betray their oaths? This should be an existential fear, not of a physical object they can encounter. Surrendering to their fear when it will cause complications to the plot is worth a [[Reroll]]. As well, making a major sacrifice to overcome it is worth a [[Reroll]].

- To die alone and unremarked.
- To be hated or reviled.
- To lose those I’ve sworn to protect.
- To be betrayed by those I trusted.

# [[Ancestry]]

The [[Character]]'s Ancestry provides physical [[Feature]]s to a character. These features should not be something that adds a fixed bonus to a [[Stat]] or all uses of a [[Skill]]. They should be based not on cultural or education provided [[Feature]]s, but just those that come from their biology or supernatural powers. [[Character]]s gain 2 [[Feature]]s from their Ancestry. It is assumed that the [[Character]] is within an average range of height making them [[Medium]] [[Size]]d and have a [[Stride]] of 6.

> ###### Small Stature
> 
> For creatures that are half the size or lower of the average height of the rest of the [[Character]]s and [[Antagonist]]s they gain the Small Stature Feature in addition to the 2 other Features their Ancestry provides.

_Examples:_

- **Coordinated** When performing a [[Maneuver]] without movement, Stride 3 spaces. 
- **Keen Senses** Add a D4 to [[Senses]] [[Roll]]s to see a [[Hidden]] foe.
- **Dim Light Vision** Can see in [[Dim Light]] as if it were [[Bright Light]].
- **Luck** As a Reaction add a D4 to a [[Roll]].
- **Mechanical Mind** Gain an [[Edge]] on [[Mind]] [[Roll]]s.
- **Overwhelming Blows** For [[Feature]]s that use [[Strength]] and deal [[Damage]], as a Perk add [[Body]] bonus to [[Damage]].
- **Recuperative** When recovering HP, recover an additional 1D4 HP.
- **Relentless** When reduced to 0 HP, reduce to 1 instead. This can be done once before taking a [[Day's Rest]].
- **Stout Physique** Gain an [[Edge]] on [[Body]] [[Roll]]s.
- **Small Stature** Small creatures can [[Stride]] 5 spaces and gain [[Cover]] when [[Screened]] behind a [[Medium]] sized or larger creature.
- **Vanish** Add a D4 to [[Stealth]] [[Roll]]s to Hide.

# [[Culture]]

A [[Character]] is inextricably tied to the cultural milieu which they were brought up within. The culture imparts values, incentives, and expectations to the [[Character]]. Mechanically this can be treated to as an [[Expertise]] to tasks that the average member of the culture should be well-versed in. As well, each culture can bestow a [[Feature]] or an [[Expertise]] of greater specificity.

# [[Background]]

What did the [[Character]] do before they became an Adventurer? This can also be used as an Expertise when performing tasks within the domain of the Background. Some Backgrounds may  grant an [[Expertise]] that specifies the scope of the Background.

# [[Archetype]]

Each character has an Archetype that outlines the core fantasy the [[Player]] is seeking. It provides [[Feature]]s that fit within that fantasy and encourage play that fulfills its promise. Archetypes should not purely take on a single role in the party, but be able to take on different roles to solve problems cooperatively be they puzzles, difficult tasks, or combat.

All the Archetype's [[Feature]]s use one of two "Core" [[Stat]]s. It also provides at least three [[Skill]]s, and an additional [[Skill]] of the [[Player]]'s choice. Depending on the themes of the Archetype, it may provide additional skills or dictate fewer [[Skill]]s providing more choice. Each Archetype should provide 2 [[Feature]]s, the [[Character]]'s [[Base HP]] and the equipment their [[Feature]]s use and that fit the Archetype's fantasy.

> The more flexible, [[Proficient]], or [[Feature]] rich a [[Character]] is, the lower their [[Base HP]] tends to be.

# [[Feature]]s

Features are special actions and abilities that [[Character]]s can perform that are thematically tied to their [[Archetype]], [[Ancestry]], [[Culture]], or [[Background]]. They can learn more Features outside of those sources, but they may not be as effective as those from their [[Archetype]]. 

## Act Types

Typically Features are things that the [[Character]] can act upon in a [[Conflict]] by making an [[Action]], [[Maneuver]], [[Response]], [[Interaction]], or [[Prolonged Act]]. Out of [[Conflict]] they can be a [[Prolonged Act]], take place during a [[Breather]], a [[Day's Rest]], effect travel, or even provide a new [[Downtime Activity]]. 

- [[Action]] Primary activity during a [[Character]]'s [[Turn]] in a [[Conflict]].
- [[Maneuver]] Something that can be done while moving or in place of movement in a [[Conflict]] that doesn't rise to an [[Action]].
- [[Response]] Can be done at any time, interrupting another [[Creature]]'s [[Turn]] if [[Trigger]]ed.
- [[Interaction]] Does not require focus to do in a [[Conflict]], can take seconds, typically talking, drawing a weapon, or interacting with a [[Terrain Feature]].
- [[Prolonged Act]] Takes a minute or more and requires an [[Action]] every [[Round]] to maintain in a [[Conflict]].
- [[Breather]] An hour wherein the [[Character]] does not make a [[Defense]] or a [[Strength]], [[Dexterity]], or [[Fortitude]] Roll.
- [[Day's Rest]] Taken once a day while the characters are resting. Is typically a low impact activity like studying or simple crafting.
- [[Downtime]] Done between [[Adventure]]s that take a week of concerted effort.

Features have a [[Stat]] + [[Skill]] that is [[Roll]]ed to achieve the Feature's listed effect. Depending on the nature of the Feature, an [[Expertise]] bonus may apply. 

> ###### [[Charge]]s
> 
> Some [[Feature]]s have a limited amount of Charges. These typically come from a [[Kit]], [[Empowered Item]], or some other outside force beyond the [[Character]]. Some Charges reset on a [[Day's Rest]] or [[Breather]]. Others require the purchase of materials or replacements when out of Charges.

## Versus

Features generally require a [[Roll]] against something to determine their outcome.
### [[Defense]]

If the Feature [[Target]]s another [[Creature]] -- especially in a non-beneficial way -- it will have a [[Defense]] it will target. [[Character]]s roll against that [[Defense]] to determine if the Feature [[Success|Succeeds]] or [[Failure|Fails]].

- **[[Body]]** Resists poisons, diseases, and other afflictions. 
- **[[Mind]]** Resists attacks on the mind.
- **[[React]]** The ability for the character to react to threats.
- **[[Deflect]]** How good the character can avoid attacks.

### [[Burn]]

Some Features take their toll on the [[Character]]. In that case the [[Roll]] is also against the [[Character]]'s current Burn. If the [[Roll]] is below the current Burn, the Feature fails even if it beats the [[Target]]'s [[Defense]]. Features that interact with Burn typically increment it up every time they are used.  [[Tier]] 1 [[Character]]s start with a [[Base Burn]] of 6 that the Burn resets to on a [[Day's Rest]].

> ###### Burn Perk
> 
> As a Perk for all Burn Features using the Feature does not increase Burn.

### [[Target Number]]

Some Features -- especially those involving crafting -- may require a set Target Number to [[Roll]] against. These [[Feature]]s don't directly [[Target]] another [[Creature]] and if they do they set the [[Potency]] which the [[Creature]] [[Defense|Defend]]s against.

> ###### [[Diminishing TN]]
> If a Feature with [[Potency]] [[Target]]s an [[Antagonist]], the [[Potency]] becomes a Diminishing TN that reduces by 1 at the beginning of the [[Antagonist]]'s [[Turn]] until it drops below a [[Defense]].

## Effect

Every Feature has an effect on the game somehow. It could do [[Damage]], apply a [[Condition]], perform a task, or otherwise have an effect on the [[Set Piece]]s or [[Creature]]s.

### [[Exploit]]

If a [[Feature]] has an Exploit effect, then when that Feature is used on an [[Exposed]] [[Target]], the Exploit effect is applied as well as the normal effect.

## Perk

Every Feature has a Perk associated with it. Some Features may have multiple Perks available for the [[Player]] to choose from. In that case, only one [[Perk]] effect can be applied. 

> ###### [[Weapon Perk]]
> [[Weapon]] [[Tag]]s can grant Features that use [[Weapon Attack Roll]]s a Perk to choose from.

## [[Duration]]

Most Features's effects are immediate from when the Feature is [[Roll]]ed. If the effect is last it either lasts until the end of a [[Target]]'s turn, or the [[Roll]] becomes a [[Decreasing TN]] that is decremented at the beginning of the [[Target]]'s [[Turn]] and ends once it drops below the listed [[Defense]].

> ###### Concentration
> Some features last as long as the [[Character]] maintains Concentration. If the concentrating Character takes [[Damage]] they must make a [[Concentration Roll]] against the [[Target Number]] of the interrupting Feature.

## Costs

The [[Game Master]] determines the cost of items using these guidelines to determine the base cost:

- 1 Coin [[Utility Item]]s.
- 10 Coin Bundles of Ammunition without [[Feature]]s. [[Useful Item]]s that can have a [[Feature]] that doesn’t effect a foe, deal damage, or heal damage. [[Light Armor]]. Items that otherwise interact with rules.
- 100 Coin Items that have [[Feature]]s, deal [[Damage]], or heal [[Damage]] that are single use such as [[Consumable]] [[Empowered Item]]s. [[Medium Armor]]. [[Fine]] [[Weapon]]s.
- 1,000 Coin [[Signature Item]]s. [[Weapon]]s. Items that have [[Charge]]s. [[Kit]]s. [[Heavy Armor]] Items that are otherwise required by Features.
- 10,000 Coin. [[Empowered Item]]s

Once the base cost is determined, use a multiplier to determine the cost.

- **X2** Commonly Available Items.
- **X3** Uncommon Items. [[Kit]] with [[Charge]]s. 3 [[Empowered Item | Empowered]] [[Consumable]]s that provide a [[Feature]].
- **X4** [[Fine]] [[Weapon]]s. 
- **X5** [[Foci]].
- **X6** [[Weapon]]s.
- **X6** Items that provide a [[Feature]].
- **X7** [[Two-Handed]] [[Weapon]].
- **X8** Items that provide a [[Feature]] with [[Attune|Attunement]].
- **X Tier** of the item has a [[Tier]], multiply the cost by the [[Tier]].

## [[Carrying Capacity]]

[[Character]]s have 12 [[Slot]]s plus their Strength Modifier. Depending on the size of the item, multiple items can fill a single [[Slot]] or may take up multiple [[Slot]]s.

### [[Consumable]]s

A group of consumables take up one [[Slot]]. Depending on the size of the [[Consumable]] a single [[Slot]] can hold:

- **3 fist sized items** Vials, potions, thrown bombs
- **12 that fit in the palm** Sling stones
- **20 that can be held in a closed hand** Arrows and Crossbow Bolts

> ###### Fine
> Two of the same [[Fine]] weapons can be stored in one [[Slot]].

### [[Signature Item]]s

Every Archetype has a Signature Item that are used when attempting their [[Feature]]s, be it a sword, a wand, tool, or consumable [[Kit]]. Typically [[Feature]]s require the item to be held in at least one hand, and they are not counted against the [[Character]]'s [[Carrying Capacity]].

## [[Armor]]

Armor is used to improve a [[Character]]'s [[Deflect]] and [[Soak]] [[Defense]]s. They take up a listed amount of [[Slot]]s.

## [[Useful Item]]s

These items can be used in tasks the [[Character]]s attempt. They may be required to make a [[Proactive Roll]] to use, provide an [[Edge]] on the task, or their lack may cause a [[Hindrance]].

## [[Utility Item]]s

Items that don’t deal [[Damage]] and are not required to use a [[Feature]]. They are often [[Consumable]].
## [[Empowered Item]]s

Empowered Items provide [[Feature]]s to their wielder. These are have unlimited usage or use [[Charge]]s that reset on a [[Day's Rest]]. Each Empowered Item has a [[Tier]] where it is advised that it becomes available to the [[Character]]s.

### [[Attuned|Attunement]]

Some items require Attunement to grant the wielder their [[Feature]]s. A [[Character]] can be attuned up to their [[Tier]] in items. Unless granted an exception by the [[Game Master]], a [[Character]] can only attune to items of their [[Tier]] or lower.

### [[Legacy Item]]s

Some Empowered Items scale with the [[Attuned]] [[Character]] granting new Features at every [[Tier]]. To unlock the next Tier of a Legacy Item the [[Game Master]] may require a thematically appropriate task by the [[Character]].

### Consumables

Consumables are single use Items. While they may require a [[Weapon Attack Roll]] to apply to the consumable to a foe, they also have a [[Potency]] set when they are crafted. [[Character]]s roll their [[Defense]] against the [[Potency]] and for [[Antagonist]]s set the [[Potency]] as the [[Decreasing TN]].

## [[Kit]]s

Kits are a collection of tools and materials that act as a [[Signature Item]] used to perform [[Archetype]] [[Feature]]s or make [[Craft]] [[Roll]]s. They contain tools that can be [[Useful Item]]s and can also be the source of new [[Feature]] or modify the [[Feature]] that require a Focus or Kit. Kits have a Tier and contain 10 [[Charge]]s that need to be replenished after use, either by buying Kit or crafting one with [[Enhanced Material]]s gathered on [[Adventure]]s. Kits can only be used to in [[Archetype]] [[Feature]]s of the same [[Tier]] and lower. The cost of a higher [[Tier]] Kit the cost multiplied by the [[Tier]] of the Kit.  

## [[Foci]]

Foci are [[Signature Item]]s are used to perform [[Archetype]] [[Feature]]s and use [[Enigma]]s. They do not have limits on their use, unless they are an [[Empowered Item]] that has [[Feature]]s with [[Charge]]s. Then those [[Feature]]s are limited by the Focus's [[Charge]]s. 

> ###### [[Enigma]]
> An Enigma is typically a supernatural power that a [[Character]] can harness. Spells, miracles, and other things that defy logical explanation qualify.  


## [[Codex]]

 A Codex is a [[Signature Item]] that holds 100 pages of [[Enigmas]] or [[Formula]]e. It can be used as a [[Focus]] but only during a [[Prolonged Act]] unless it is an [[Empowered Item]] that allows it to be used for [[Action]]s and [[Maneuver]]s.
 

> ###### [[Formula]]
> 
> A Formula is instructions to create an [[Empowered Item]], be it from a mundane source or a supernatural one.


## Weapons

[[Feature]]s can include a [[Weapon Attack]]. Weapons are described by [[Tag]]s that detail how they interact with the game. These tags provide the rules and [[Perk]]s that apply while being used.

To perform them a [[Character]] must be wielding a weapon and make a [[Strength]] + [[Weapons]] [[Weapon Attack Roll]] to deal 1D6, unless the [[Weapon]]'s [[Tag]]s override that. The wielder [[Threatened|Threaten]]s [[Adjacent]] [[Target]]s. Using a [[Weapon]] without being [[Proficient]] in the [[Weapons]] [[Skill]] has a [[Hindrance]].

> ###### [[Weapon Strike]]
> 
> When wielding a [[Weapon]] the [[Character]] has the Weapon Strike [[Feature]]. As an [[Action]] make a [[Weapon Attack]] [[Roll]] against a [[Target]]'s [[Deflect]] to deal 1D6 [[Damage]].

### Weapon [[Tag]]s

Weapon Tags describe the damage a [[Weapon]] does, what [[Perk]]s [[Weapon Attack]] [[Feature]]s have, and what [[Feature]]s the weapon may grant.

#### Size Tags

- **[[Fine]]** Small light weapons that fit in a single hand deal 1D4 [[Damage]]. As a [[Maneuver]] the wielder can make a [[Weapon Attack]] against a single [[Target]].
- **[[Two-Handed]]** Large weapons that require two hands to wield deal 1D8 [[Damage]]. When wielding a Two-Handed Weapon the wielder cannot use [[Feature]]s that require a free hand that turn.
- **[[Versatile]]** Weapons that can be used one or two handed. When wielding two-handed it deals 1D8 the wielder cannot use [[Feature]]s that require a free hand that turn. Otherwise it deals 1D6 and the wielder can use [[Feature]] that require a free hand.

#### Style Tags

- **[[Bladed]]** Uses a cutting edge to wound a [[Target]]. As a [[Weapon Perk]] the [[Target]] gains a level of [[Bleeding]].
- **[[Blunt]]** Deals blunt force trauma to a [[Target]]. As a [[Weapon Perk]] the [[Target]] is [[Blighted]].
- **[[Piercing]]** Uses a sharp tip in a jabbing motion. As a [[Weapon Perk]] the [[Damage]] bypasses the [[Target]]'s [[Soak]].

#### Feature Tags

- **[[Ammunition]]** Uses a limited pool of ammunition. Ammunition with Features also effect [[Target]]s of the [[Weapon Attack]].
- **[[Basic]]** The wielder does not have a [[Hindrance]] when not [[Proficient]] in the [[Weapons]] [[Skill]].
- **[[Clip]] #** The weapon can hold the listed number worth of Ammunition that can be used before [[Reload]]ing.
- **[[Charging]]** When used after a maneuver that includes movement, deal an additional die of the [[Weapon]]'s [[Damage]].
- **[[Disarming]]** As a [[Weapon Perk]] the [[Target]] drops a held item or weapon.
- [[Emplaced]] Uses [[Wits]] + [[Weapons]] for [[Weapon Attack Roll]]s.
- **[[Finesse]]** Uses [[Dexterity]] + [[Weapons]] for [[Weapon Attack Roll]]s with the weapon.
- **[[Heavy]]** Increase the [[Damage]] die to the next size.
- **[[Mechanical]]** Uses [[Wits]] + [[Weapons]] for [[Weapon Attack Roll]]s with the weapon.
- **[[Mounted]]** Can be used one handed while mounted even if other [[Tag]]s would prevent it.
- **[[Natural]]** Body parts that can be used as a [[Weapon]] that use [[Strength]] + [[Athletics]] for their [[Weapon Attack Roll]] and deal the listed die of damage. When disarmed the weapon is disabled until the [[Creature]] recovers all [[HP]].
- **[[Ranged]] #** Can attack a [[Target]] up to the listed [[Space]]s. Does not [[Threatened|Threaten]] [[Target]]s.
- **[[Reach]] #** [[Target]]s within the listed [[Space]]s are [[Threatened]] and can be attacked with the [[Weapon]].
- **[[Reload]]** Requires listed amount of [[Maneuver]]s to reload and fire again.
- **[[Shield]] #** The shield adds the listed value to the wielder's [[Deflect]]. As a [[Maneuver]] the wielder can make a [[Weapon Attack Roll]] to deal 1D4 [[Damage]].
- **[[Slam]]** As a [[Weapon Perk]], [[Push]] the [[Target]] the listed [[Space]]s.
- **[[Thrown]] #** Can attack a Target up to the listed [[Space]]s. [[Threatened|Threatens]] [[Adjacent]] [[Target]]s.


## [[Pet]]s

Pets act on their master’s turn and are trained to move where directed,  [[Hide]], or [[Dodge]] in combat. Commanding a pet is a [[Maneuver]] that can be done while moving. If attacked, and not given instructions otherwise, the pet will attack its attacker. Instead of being purchased they can be trained using [[Downtime]].

> ###### Training Pets for Combat
> 
> Training a pet for combat takes time and effort. Once the pet is purchased, it can be trained for combat using [[Downtime]]. To purchase a [[Battle Trained]] Pet multiply the cost of the animal by 10. 

## [[Treasure]]

Treasure are items that don't have an immediate use that the [[Character]]s find in their [[Adventure]]s.  They can be traded for other Items or services.  They take up at least 1 [[Slot]].

### [[Coin]]

Currency is expressed in Coin that abstracts the various denominations that exist within the game world. It is the most flexible Treasure that can be used to purchase items, support a Lifestyle, and gather materials to [[Craft]] items. Less than Coin 10,000 does not take up a [[Slot]] as higher denominations can be used.

# Assigning Stats

Once the [[Player]] has decided on their [[Character]]'s [[Ancestry]], [[Culture]], [[Background]], [[Archetype]], and have purchased their equipment then they should assign their [[Stat]]s to be most advantageous for using the [[Archetype]]'s [[Feature]]s and to meet the vision they have for their character. Distribute the following bonuses to each [[Stat]]:

	**+2**,	 **+2**,	 **+1**,	 **+1**,	 +0,	 -1

Once the [[Stat]]s have been set, increase one [[Stat]] by +1.

# Defenses

Most Defenses are [[Roll]]ed when the [[Character]] is the [[Target]] of another [[Feature]], be it from a foe or the [[Set Piece]]. 

- **Body** [[Strength]] + [[Fortitude]]. Resists poisons, diseases, and other afflictions. 
- **Mind** [[Aptitude]] + [[Will]]. Resists attacks on the mind.
- **React** [[Dexterity]] + [[Wits]]. The ability for the character to react to threats.
- **Deflect** [[React]] + [[Armor]]. How good the character can avoid attacks.

## [[Soak]]

When a [[Creature]] takes [[Damage]], the first thing they do is subtract their [[Soak]] from it. They cannot reduce the [[Damage]] to below 1. Their [[Soak]] is determined by totaling their [[Fortitude]] + [[Armor]]'s [[Soak]].

## HP

A [[Character]]'s HP is a measure of their health and ability to resist [[Damage]]. Their [[Archetype]] provides the [[Base HP]] which is added to the [[Character]]'s [[Fortitude]] to determine their [[Maximum HP]]. Keep a running tally of how much HP the [[Character]] has left after it takes [[Damage]]. When it drops below 0 the [[Character]] begins [[Dying]]. The [[Character]] can only be healed up to their [[Maximum HP]].

> ###### Temporary HP
> 
> Some features will provide a [[Character]] Temporary HP. This HP is reduced as the [[Character]] takes [[Damage]]. [[Damage]] to Temporary HP is not reduced by the [[Character]]'s [[Soak]]. Damage remaining after Temporary HP has been subtracted from it is [[Soak]]ed and reduces HP as normal.

# Filling Out the Character Sheet

Most aspects of the [[Character]] have a single designated areas to write them in: [[Personality Trait]]s, [[Ancestry]], [[Culture]], [[Background]], [[Stat]]s, [[Skill]]s, and [[Expertise]]s. Though it may be helpful to add your [[Culture]] and [[Background]] to your [[Expertise]]s area along with any other specific [[Expertise]]s for easy reminder that they may apply to [[Roll]]s.

[[Feature]]s are best recorded both in the Features section in full and under the specific task type with all the bonuses pre-calculated and rules simplified so fit in the [[Action]], [[Maneuver]], [[Interaction]], or [[Response]] sections. This way you have an easy reminder of what is necessary to [[Roll]] when performing these [[Feature]]s and declaring their effects when used in [[Conflict]]s. While still having an in depth description of the rules for easy reference.

If any Feature uses [[Burn]], add an area to tally what the [[Character]]'s current [[Burn]] is.

Finally, record how many [[Slot]]s for equipment the character has and list them in the Equipment section. If any item provides a [[Feature]], note it in the Features section.
