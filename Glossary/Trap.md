Traps are Terrain Features that are a [[Response]] to a [[Trigger]]. Like all [[Response]]s the [[Trigger]]  could be moving into a specific [[Space]], performing a type of [[Action]], or getting a specific result on a [[Roll]].

In a [[Chase]] any traps set along the path target the [[Lead]] before they can take a turn. If they are already aware of the Trap, they have an [[Edge]] on their [[Defense Roll]] against it. As a [[Perk]] on their [[Defense]] they avoid the Trap completely and are aware of it, gaining an [[Edge]] on [[Juke]] this [[Turn]] to direct a following participant into it.
