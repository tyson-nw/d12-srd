Foci are [[Signature Item]]s are used to perform [[Archetype]] [[Feature]]s. They do not have limits on their use, unless they are an [[Empowered Item]] that has [[Feature]]s with [[Charge]]s. Then those [[Feature]]s are limited by the Focus's [[Charge]]s.
