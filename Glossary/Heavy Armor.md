Wearing Heavy Armor makes the [[Creature]]'s Deflect +4 regardless of [[React]], and increases [[Soak]] by 5. It takes up 2 [[Slot]]s. When worn the wearer Cannot use [[Burn]] [[Feature]]s and a [[Hindrance]] to [[Stealth]] or [[Dexterity]] [[Roll]]s.
