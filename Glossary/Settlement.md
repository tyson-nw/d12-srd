Settlements are built out communities within certain ranges of sizes that can provide services and shelter to [[Character]]s. They can support only up to a specific level of [[Lifestyle]]s and [[Downtime Activity|Downtime Activities]]. The four Settlement sizes are : [[Village]], [[Town]], [[City]], [[Metropolis]]
