Each character has an Archetype that outlines the core fantasy the [[Player]] is seeking, it provides [[Feature]]s that fit within that fantasy and encourage play that fulfills its promise. Archetypes should not purely take on a single role in the party, but be able to take on different roles to solve problems cooperatively be they puzzles, difficult tasks, or combat.
