Blight targets a single [[Stat]] of the [[Character]] for a number of levels. All [[Roll]]s that include the Blighted [[Stat]] subtract a D4 from that roll for each level of Blight. If a Character has more blight in one [[Stat]] as 2 plus their [[Fortitude]] [[Stat]] they become [[Incapacitated]]. For every Blight applies to an [[Antagonist]], reduce the [[Target Number]]s of [[Feature]]s and [[Defense]]s by 1.
