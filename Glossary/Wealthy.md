This [[Lifestyle Expense]] requires the employment of others to maintain a high quality of food, clothing, and housing that can impress guests. [[Social]] and [[Stealth]] [[Roll]]s to pass among Poor individuals have a [[Hindrance]] during the next [[Downtime]] or [[Adventure]]. [[Social]] [[Roll]]s against a Poor individual has an [[Edge]]. Costs 1,000 [[Coin]] per [[Downtime]], 500 [[Coin]] a day when traveling.
