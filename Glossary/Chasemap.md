A Chasemap is a grid with a [[Lead]] position in the middle of one edge and at least 15 [[Space]]s deep with a line down the center that describes how far the participants are from the [[Lead]]. Each [[Token]] takes up their normal space along the center [[Lane]]. On either side of the center [[Lane]] are the amount of [[Lane]]s that the chase's environment will allow characters to be the same distance form the [[Lead]].
