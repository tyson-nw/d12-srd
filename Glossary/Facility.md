A Facility is a static location that contains the tools and mechanisms necessary to craft complex items. Forges, Armories, Alchemy Labs, and Scriptorium are examples of Facilities. [[Settlement]]s are limited to how many [[Facility|Facilities]] they can support.

| Settlement         | Max Facilites |
| :----------------- | :------------ |
| **[[Village]]**    | 1             |
| **[[Town]]**       | 3             |
| **[[City]]**       | 9             |
| **[[Metropolis]]** | 27+           |
