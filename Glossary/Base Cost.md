The base cost of an item depending on their category or [[Tag]]s.

---

- **1 Coin** [[Utility Item]]s.
- **10 Coin** Bundles of Ammunition without [[Feature]]s. [[Useful Item]]s that can have a [[Feature]] that doesn’t effect a foe, deal damage, or heal damage. [[Light Armor]]. Items that otherwise interact with rules.
- **100 Coin** Items that have [[Feature]]s, deal [[Damage]], or heal [[Damage]] that are single use such as [[Consumable]] [[Empowered Item]]s. [[Medium Armor]]. [[Fine]] [[Weapon]]s.
- **1,000 Coin** [[Signature Item]]s. [[Weapon]]s. Items that have [[Charge]]s. [[Kit]]s. [[Heavy Armor]] Items that are otherwise required by Features.
- **10,000 Coin** [[Empowered Item]]s
