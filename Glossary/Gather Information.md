To find information on a person, place, or thing the characters need to make a [[Complex Roll]] against a [[Target Number]] based on how secret or obscure the information is with an [[Aptitude]] + [[Social]] to find who or where the information might be, a [[Dexterity]] + [[Stealth]] to sneak in or a [[Will]] + [[Stealth]] to fake their way to get the knowledge, and a [[Will]] + [[Social]] to convince someone to give the knowledge or a [[Aptitude]] + [[Senses]] to extract the knowledge from documents. On a [[Success]] the [[Character]]s learn three facts about the target. As a [[Perk]] the characters gain two more facts. As a [[Complication]] they get caught if it is secured knowledge or learn an additional falsehood if it is not. On a [[Failure]] they find nothing.
