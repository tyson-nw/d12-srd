An abstraction of the amount of food and water it takes to sustain a [[Character]] for a day. While mechanically it is expended during the [[Day's Rest]] it is consumed the following day. It takes up 1 Space, costs 1 [[Coin]], and requires cooking to be effective. A Preserved Day's Ration does not require cooking and costs 3 [[Coin]].
