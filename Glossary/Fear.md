What does the [[Character]] fear? What will dissuade them from their goal? What holds them back and betray their oaths? This should be an existential fear, not of a physical object they can encounter. Surrendering to their fear when it will cause complications to the plot is worth a [[Reroll]]. As well, making a major sacrifice to overcome it is worth a [[Reroll]].
