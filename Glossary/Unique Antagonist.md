[[Leader]]s, [[Solo]]s, and other one-off [[Antagonist]]s can be Unique Antagonists. They are more developed than other [[Antagonist]]s, having multiple often contradicting [[Themes]]. Their [[Motivation]]s and [[Goal]]s should be more developed and specific. They tend to have more [[Feature]]s available to them with higher [[Target Number]]s that can be more diverse thematically and more complex mechanically. If they are encountered within an environment that they have control of, they can have additional [[Lair Feature]]s that give them advantage in a conflict. Luring the Antagonist out of their lair should be an option for the [[Player]]s to have their [[Character]]s attempt.
