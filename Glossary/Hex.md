A Hex is a 3mi by 3mi hexagon that abstracts a section of terrain. Every Hex has a [[Target Number ]]used by the [[Navigation Roll|Navigation]] and [[Survey Roll]]s, and a level of [[Familiarity]]. They should have at leat 1 [[Set Piece]] that the [[Character]]s can repeatedly visit and use as a landmark.
