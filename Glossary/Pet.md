Pets act on their master’s turn and are trained to move where directed,  [[Hide]], or [[Dodge]] in combat. Commanding a pet is a [[Maneuver]] that can be done while moving. If attacked, and not given instructions otherwise, the pet will attack its attacker. Instead of being purchased they can be trained using [[Downtime]].
