How the Antagonist relates to the [[Character]]s is their Demeanor. Through interactions they may change the Demeanor up and down the ranks of the following scale.

- **Allied** They will risk themselves for the Characters. They have a -4 to Mind defense against [[Character]] Social Rolls.
- **Supportive** They will offer support that doesn’t put them at risk. They have a -2 to their Mind defense against [[Character]] Social Rolls.
- **Indifferent** They will not go out of their way to support or hinder the [[Character]]s.
- **Quarrelsome** They will do what they can to hinder the [[Character]]s without putting themselves at risk. They have a +3 on their Mind defense against [[Character]]'s Social Rolls to convince them to help the [[Character]]s.
- **Hostile** They are willing to put their lives on the line to hurt the [[Character]]s. They have a +6 on their Mind Defense against [[Character]]'s Social Rolls to convince them to help.
