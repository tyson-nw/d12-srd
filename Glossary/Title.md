Tiles are ephemeral awards that act as an Expertise in a specific situation. These should be thematically tied to the adventure and they don't have to be permanent. They can use the Title to gain an [[Edge]] on a related [[Roll]] 3 times before losing it. Or the [[Character]]'s actions or behavior can lose them a title. Negative titles are possible and may give a -2 bonus to specific situations. And some titles may positive and negative bonuses tied to different situations.
