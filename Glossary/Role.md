If a Conflict devolves into combat, Antagonists will take on a Role that -- combined with their [[Themes]] --  guides what [[Defense]]s, [[Feature]]s, and [[Target Number]] they have available to them. They inform where the Antagonist is most effective in combat, what their strengths are, and their weaknesses.

- **[[Ambusher]]** Uses stealth and being hidden to gain advantage.
- **[[Artillery]]** Stays to the edge of the battlefield firing in with ranged attacks.
- **[[Bruiser]]** Holds their own, but work best with followers and a leader.
- **[[Horde]]** Horde creatures are designed to work in coordination with other Horde creatures but are weak on their own.
- **[[Leader]]** Leads groups of Antagonists. They enhance and amplify the abilities of their followers commanding them to greater effectiveness.
- **[[Skirmisher]]** Mobile and versatile the Skirmisher moves through the battlefield supporting their allies with both melee and ranged attacks.
- **[[Solo]]** Can stand on their own against the [[Character]]s with minimal support. They often can take multiple [[Action]]s or [[Response]]s.
- **[[Follower]]** Filling the ranks of the Antagonists they work best when commanded by a [[Leader]], [[Solo]], or [[Bruiser]].
