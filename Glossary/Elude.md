As an [[Action]] the participant attempts to be harder to hit. On-foot participants use [[Dexterity]] and Mounted participants use the [[Mount]]'s [[Dexterity]] in their [[Chase Roll]]. On a [[Success]] the participant, [[Mount]], [[Vehicle]], and passengers gain an [[Edge]] on [[Deflect]] and [[React]] [[Defense]]s.
