Some Features take their toll on the [[Character]]. In that case the [[Roll]] is also against the [[Character]]'s current Burn. If the [[Roll]] is below the current Burn, the Feature fails even if it beats the [[Target]]'s [[Defense]]. Features that interact with Burn typically increment it up every time they are used.  [[Tier]] 1 [[Character]]s start with a [[Base Burn]] of 6 that the Burn resets to on a [[Day's Rest]].
