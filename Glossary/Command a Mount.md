As a Maneuver a character can command a [[Mount]] to move, [[Hide]], or [[Dodge]]. If the [[Mount]] is [[Battle Trained]] it may also be commanded to Attack or Assist. Without a command a [[Mount]] will attempt to preserve its life by [[Dodge|Dodging]] and staying in place if not threatened, or moving out of combat if they are. If commanded, they will either stay put and Dodge or move where commanded while Evading.
