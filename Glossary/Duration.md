Most Features's effects are immediate from when the [[Feature]] is [[Roll]]ed. If it has a Duration then:
- The effect is lasts until the end of a [[Target]]'s turn. 
- The [[Roll]] becomes a [[Decreasing TN]] that is decremented at the beginning of the [[Target]]'s [[Turn]] and ends once it drops below the listed [[Defense]],
- The [[Feature]] has a set time limit it may require [[Concentration]] to maintain for the listed time limit.
