Kits are a collection of tools and materials that act as a [[Signature Item]] used to perform [[Archetype]] [[Feature]]s or make [[Craft]] [[Roll]]s. They contain tools that can be [[Useful Item]]s and can also be the source of new [[Feature]] or modify the [[Feature]] that require a Focus or Kit. Kits have a Tier and contain 10 [[Charge]]s that need to be replenished after use, either by buying Kit or crafting one with [[Enhanced Material]]s gathered on [[Adventure]]s. Kits can only be used to in [[Archetype]] [[Feature]]s of the same [[Tier]] and lower. The cost of a higher [[Tier]] Kit the cost multiplied by the [[Tier]] of the Kit.
