[[Chase Roll]]s have the same scale as a [[Proactive Roll]]. Chases have their own list of [[Chase Complication]]]]s for the [[Game Master]] to choose from.

 - **12 on Die and over [[Target Number|TN]]** [[Success]] with a [[Perk]].
 - **Over or equal to [[Target Number|TN]]** [[Success]].
 - **Under [[Target Number|TN]]** [[Success]] with a [[Chase Complication]].
 - **1 on the Die** [[Failure]] and the Character Falls Behind equal to the [[Lead]]'s movement.
