Once the [[Character]] has enough [[Research]] for an [[Enigma]] they can Transcribe it to their [[Codex]]. As a [[Complication]] the Character needs to research an additional 1/10 of the cost to be able to transcribe the [[Enigma]]. On a [[Failure]] the [[Character]] needs an additional 1/2 of the [[Research Cost]] to attempt to Transcribe the [[Enigma]] again. Each additional failure reduces the [[Research Cost ]]by 1/2 of the previous attempt.
