Currency is expressed in Coin that abstracts the various denominations that exist within the game world. It is the most flexible Treasure that can be used to purchase items, support a [[Lifestyle]], and gather materials to [[Craft]] items. Less than Coin 10,000 does not take up a [[Slot]] as higher denominations can be used.
