During a [[Breather]] a character can use a charge of a Healers [[Kit]] to make an [[Aptitude]] + [[Knowledge]] [[Proactive Roll]] against a creature's current HP. As a [[Success]] all [[Bleeding]] conditions end at the beginning of the Breather and the [[Creature]] heals 1d6 [[HP]] at the end of the Breather. As a [[Perk]] it heals 6 HP instead. As a [[Complication]] the creature is [[Blighted]]. And as a [[Failure]] they do not heal at all and the Medical Kit [[Charge]] is wasted.
