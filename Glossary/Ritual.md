Enigmas with a Ritual casting time can be cast with a [[Prolonged Act]] over a period of time to prevent having to roll against the Enigma's [[Burn]]. Multiple [[Character]]s can be led in the ritual if they are [[Proficient]] in [[Enigmas]]. However, the [[Character]] leading the Ritual can only perform it if their Burn is less than 12. And if a participant takes damage they must make a [[Concentration Roll]] or the Ritual loses the benefit of their participation. Depending on the [[Tier]] of the Enigma, the amount of participants may grant an [[Edge]] to the lead caster's [[Casting Roll]].

| Tier | Ritual Time | For Edge |
| :--: | :---------: | :------: |
|  1   | 10 minutes  |    3     |
|  2   | 30 minutes  |    6     |
|  3   |   1 hour    |    9     |
|  4   |   4 hours   |    12    |
