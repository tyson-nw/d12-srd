Roll a D12 plus the [[Character]]'s [[Fortitude]] against [[Target Number]] 8. If they [[Roll]] three [[Success]]es before three [[Failure]]s the [[Character]] stops [[Dying]]. If they take [[Damage]] they gain one [[Failure]], if the [[Damage]] is greater than [[Character]]' the [[Maximum HP]] they gain an additional [[Failure]].
- **Roll a 12 on the D12** Two [[Success]]es.
- **Roll over or equal to the TN** One [[Success]].
- **Roll under the TN** One [[Failure]].
- **Roll a 1 on the D12** Two [[Failure]]s.
