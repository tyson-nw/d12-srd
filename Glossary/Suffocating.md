A [[Creature]] can suffocate for a number of rounds equal to their [[Body]]. After that they begin making [[Stabilization Roll]]s at the beginning of they turn for as long as they are Suffocating. As a [[Perk]] they turn one [[Failure]] into a [[Success]]. Once they [[Failure|Fail]] three total [[Stabilization Rolls]] they die.
