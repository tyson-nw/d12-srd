Random Encounters can break up travel and provide an interesting encounter for the [[Character]]s to overcome. As these are world-building aids, it is appropriate for the [[Game Master]] to roll them, but having the players roll for their own potential doom can be fun. When rolling a Random Encounter, rolling twice on the table and combine the outputs to produce a more dynamic encounters with two [[Antagonist]]s that can be in [[Conflict]] with each other and the Characters than just rolling once for one [[Antagonist]]. Depending on the relative danger or safety of the terrain either adds or subtracts a modifier from the roll, as can the [[Navigation Roll]]. Modifiers cannot lower the roll below 1.
