The Grabbed creature's movement is reduced to 0 and their [[Weapon Attack Roll]]s have a [[Hindrance]] unless using a [[Weapons]] with the [[Fine]] [[Tag]]. Grabbed creatures are [[Exposed]]. As an [[Action]] the Grabbed [[Creature]] can attempt to escape by comparing their [[Dexterity]] + [[Athletics]] or [[Strength]] + [[Athletics]] to the a new [[Roll]] of the [[Feature]] that grabbed them. If a [[Feature]] would move a [[Grabbed]] [[Creature]] then compare it's [[Roll]] or [[Target Number]] to the [[Roll]] or [[Target Number]] of the [[Feature]] that Grabbed them.
