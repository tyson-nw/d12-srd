When talking to an Antagonist, [[Character]]s can make a [[Wits]] + [[Social]] [[Roll]] against the Antagonist's [[Mind]] [[Defense]]. On a [[Success]] they learn either their [[Motivation]] or [[Goal]]. As a [[Perk]] they learn both, and as a complication they learn both but the [[Motivation]] or [[Goal]] is inaccurate. On a [[Failure]] they learn nothing. This should be paired with the [[Player]]s describing how they are getting the information or roleplaying as their [[Character]]s in conversation with the Antagonist.
