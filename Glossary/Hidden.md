A [[Character]] becomes hidden if they can make a foe unaware of them. Once behind cover, as an [[Action]] they can make a [[Wits]] + [[Stealth]] [[Roll]] against their observer's [[Mind]] and move up to half their [[Stride]] their observer will not notice the [[Character]] changed position. The [[Character]] will remain hidden until they draw attention to themself by using a [[Feature]] that deals Damage or makes noise. [[Creature]]s unaware of the hidden [[Character]] are [[Exposed]] to that [[Creature]].
