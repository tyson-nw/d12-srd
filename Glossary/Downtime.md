When [[Character]]'s are not adventuring, out of [[Conflict]], and living their lives they can take Downtime Activities. Downtime Activities take a week and have a cost associated with the [[Character]]'s [[Lifestyle]]. Typically Downtime Activities involve a [[Complex Roll]], it's Target Number is determined by the Characters' [[Lifestyle]], the [[Tier]] of the [[Challenge]], or the [[Challenge Rating]] of a target [[Creature]]. Multiple Characters can participate together in the same Downtime Activity unless the Activity states otherwise, either to make a Roll or assist in a Roll. Some Activities may have consequences, those consequences that lead to [[Conflict]]s do not interrupt the Downtime, but can create [[Conflict]]s at the end of all the [[Character]]'s the Downtime.
