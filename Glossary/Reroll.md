By playing to a [[Character]]'s Personality Traits, the [[Game Master]] rewards [[Reroll]]s that can be spent during that session. When the [[Character]] is embodying the trait, the [[Player]] can make an aside comment indicating that they are playing to the [[Drive]], [[Flaw]], or [[Fear]].
