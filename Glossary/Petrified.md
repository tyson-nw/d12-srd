Petrified [[Creature]]s and their held items are changed to an inanimate substance. The creature cannot make an [[Action]], make a [[Maneuver]], make a [[Response]], move, or speak. They also have [[Durability]], and are immune to poison, disease, and aging. Poisons and diseases are not neutralized, just suspended. Petrified creatures are [[Exposed]].
