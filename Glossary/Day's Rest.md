Day's Rests are a total of 10 hours without [[Conflict]] in which the characters rest and do light activity like setting up camp, cooking, sleeping, light exercise, and reading. At least 6 of those hours need to be a [[Comfortable Rest]]. The rest can be taken up by watches, crafting, or researching.
