As a Prolonged Action the characters can carefully search a moderately sized room with 3 [[Scene Feature]]s in 10 minutes. Each Up to three [[Character]]s make an [[Aptitude]]+[[Senses]] [[Complex Roll]] each against the highest TN of the [[Scene Feature]]s. The remaining Characters can assist if they are Proficient in [[Senses]] or have an applicable [[Expertise]]. On 3 [[Success]]es the Characters find all 3 Scene Features. On 2 Successes they find 2. On 1 Success they find 1 and trigger 1 hostile feature or cause some other [[Complication]]. With 0 Successes they discover no features and all hostile features are triggered.
