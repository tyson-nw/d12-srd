Scene Features are aspects of the scene that a [[Character]] or [[Antagonist]] can use to perform a [[Feature]]. Typically [[Scene Feature]]s can be used with an [[Interaction]] or [[Maneuver]] to manipulate the source of the [[Feature]]. For [[Feature]]s with drastic consequences they may require multiple [[Action]]s performed by multiple [[Character]]s or [[Antagonist]]s to activate.
