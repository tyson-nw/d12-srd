A Noble [[Lifestyle Expense]] requires the employment of others and maintenance of a territory with regular expenditures on food, clothing, and accessories that impress the local community. [[Social]] and [[Stealth]] [[Roll]]s to pass as Poor or Comfortable individuals have a [[Hindrance]] during the next [[Downtime]] or [[Adventure]]. [[Social]] [[Roll]]s against a [[Poor]] or [[Comfortable]] individual has an [[Edge]]. Typically requires a [[Character]] to be a member of the nobility to maintain this [[Lifestyle]]. Costs 10,000 [[Coin]] per [[Downtime]], 5,000 [[Coin]] a day when traveling.
