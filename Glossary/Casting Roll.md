To manifest an Enigma the [[Character]] make a [[Casting Stat]] + [[Enigmas]] [[Roll]]. If the effect negatively impacts the [[Target]] of the Enigma, the [[Roll]] is against the Enigma's listed [[Defense]]. If the [[Enigma Feature]] uses [[Burn]] that same [[Roll]] must also meet or surpass the [[Character]]'s current [[Burn]] to manifest.

-  **12 on Die and over [[Defense]] and [[Burn]]** [[Success|Succeeds]] with a [[Perk]].
- **Over or equal to [[Defense]]** and **[[Burn]]** [[Success|Succeeds]].
- **Under [[Defense]]** [[Failure|Fails]].
- **Under [[Burn]]** [[Failure|Fails]] and does not increase [[Burn]].
- **1 on the Die** [[Failure|Fails]] and does increase [[Burn]].
