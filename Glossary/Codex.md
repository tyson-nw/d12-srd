A Codex is a [[Signature Item]] that holds 100 pages of [[Enigma]]s or [[Formula]]e. It can be used as a [[Focus]] but only during a [[Prolonged Act]] unless it is an [[Empowered Item]] that allows it to be used for [[Action]]s and [[Maneuver]]s.
