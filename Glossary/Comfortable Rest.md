A Comfortable Rest requires protection to stay and warm, not wearing armor, and a [[Day's Ration]] worth of food and water. To negate cold weather they will need a Bedroll. To negate rain or snow when they don’t have shelter they will need a Tent or construct shelter. To cook a Day's Ration requires a fire (Day's Rations that don't require a fire cost 3 times the Coin). If all three can't be met, the [[Character]] gains a [[Blighted|Blight]]. If the character does not expend a [[Day's Ration]], they are [[Sickened]] the next day.
